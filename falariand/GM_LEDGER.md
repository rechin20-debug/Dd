# GM LEDGER — Falariand campaign (private world state)

*Updated at the turn of the year, end of Deepdark 812 S.C. Next update: end of Deepdark 813.*

## Rules of play
- Natural/real-world resolution, no stats. Outcomes from plausibility, skill, preparation, circumstance.
- World moves on its own. NPCs pursue goals offscreen. Player is NOT the center.
- Protagonists of the age: Ilsabet, Corwen, Aila, Tove Ranne, Brum Whitemaw, Ilvai Dhuun. Player = Evander, side figure. No protagonist is a villain; Wadun's view of the south is biased.
- NO PLOT HOOKS OR RAILROADING AIMED AT EVANDER. No mysterious notes, summons, chosen-one signs, or NPCs seeking him out for plot reasons. He only meets the plot if he goes to it. NPCs interact with him only as their own lives naturally would (bank, Academy, acquaintances, family in ordinary ways).
- Lethal choices kill. Bad plans fail. Generosity and threats both have consequences.
- Ledger is updated once per in-game year, not after every action. Small actions are tracked in play.
- Ruins vary: many hold nothing, or mundane danger (bad air, collapse, weather, animals, people). Don't repeat the same monster type.

## Geography & scale (world = 1.5x Earth diameter; R 9,556 km; 166.8 km per degree; ~Earth-normal gravity)
- Source map: `maps/world_map.webp`, equirectangular, 0.18° per px (30 km N-S per px). E-W stretched ~4.4x at Falariand's latitude.
- Wadun 76.6N, ~83 km from the Thornmarch border. Northern border/Ten Keeps ~82N, ~884 km north of Wadun (4-6 weeks overland).
- Crown lands ~650k km2, ~2M people. Wadun ~60k people.
- Rime Duchies: bordered cluster W of the crown lands. 14 states, ~610k km2, ~600k people.
- Thornmarch: bordered cluster E of Wadun, Silkwater at its tip. ~15 states, ~1.05M km2, ~3M people.
- Silkwater enclave ~3-5k km2.
- Gravesmere: ~860k km2 island, 65.6-73.7N, nearest point ~710 km from Wadun (4-6 days sail). Royal barrows on the north shore. Falar's true barrow lies ~26 days' walk inland. The black border lines on the map are the old lines of a fallen realm; the interior is wild and forbidden.
- Travel: skis/sledge in snow ~25-30 km/day; foot in mud ~15-20; Fell pony on firm summer road ~30-35; loaded ~15.

## SNOWFLAKE (bound ice elemental, frost-kin)
- **Bargain (9 Rimewane 812, Primordial):**
  - 5 winters bound in the rimeglass lantern; then Evander takes it north to the deep cold and it chooses to stay or go.
  - It sees the world (not kept in darkness).
  - Never drained.
  - It MAY send its cold if Evander is in peril (withholding is not harm).
  - It must not harm him; if it is responsible for his death, it is chained to the vessel forever.
  - If he dies otherwise, it is free.
  - If he knowingly breaks the bargain, it is free at once and unbindable by him.
- **Added terms:**
  - **Broken-vessel term** (sealed 27 Rimewane): only into ice, rimeglass or clear glass; if Evander is absent, into the nearest such thing to wait; the winters keep counting; a proper vessel within one moon or it goes free and north.
  - **Death term** (sworn before Fenwick): if freed by Evander's death, it goes north and harms no one, unless someone tries to bind or harm it.
- **Registered** with the Academy, 15 Thawmonth. Conditions: housed and locked when not in hand; annual re-inspection (due ~Thawmonth 813); reclassification as it grows; liability for its acts is Evander's.
- **Line Evander set:** only death in the next minute or breath. Snowflake chose, freely, to always act inside that line for him, and never as a weapon for war.
- **Promises Evander made:** never take it to the weeping ice; never point the Lantern-ward at it.
- **Perceives** through the vessel (light, sound, movement, cold, draught); **cannot smell or taste.**
- **Growing:** stronger in deep cold, polar night and near rimeglass; very bright this Deepdark.
- **Keeps hidden:**
  - its true name
  - the full meaning of its **humming** (it answers the "mother-ice"; this year that voice is "wrong, weeping," afraid of something under the giant)
  - what it knows of the north
- **Its view of Evander:** "keeps his word, careless with himself, I would like him not to die." Hasn't decided whether it will stay after five winters. Opposes Evander joining the army.

## Salvage law (Falariand)
- Old ruins on Crown waste or unowned land: salvage tolerated.
- Private land: the landowner's.
- Treasure trove (hidden gold/silver, owner unknown): Crown; a reporting finder is rewarded.
- Contraband: forfeit; a reporting finder gets ~1/3.
- Live claims only where actively kept: Academy (recent inventories; active seals), Church (interdicts; parish property), administered Crown land.
