# LOTR Roleplay — Campaign Log

Persistent record of the simulation. Facts established in play override canon from the point they occur.

## Rules (from player)
- Real consequences, no plot armor. Player character and NPCs die if their actions lead to it.
- World does not revolve around the PC. Canon timeline proceeds on its own unless PC actions change it.
- No lucky breaks, no grand conspiracies, no ancient-mystery hooks.
- NPCs speak and act per canon personality; varied voices.
- No exaggeration. Plausible failure is allowed and expected.
- GM narrates the world and NPCs only; never acts or speaks for the PC.
