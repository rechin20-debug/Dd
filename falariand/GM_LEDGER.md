# GM LEDGER — Falariand campaign (private world state)

## Rules of play
- Natural/real-world resolution, no stats. Outcomes from plausibility, skill, preparation, circumstance.
- World moves on its own. NPCs pursue goals offscreen. Player is NOT the center.
- Protagonists of the age: Ilsabet (heir) and Corwen (bastard). Player = Evander, side figure.
- NO PLOT HOOKS OR RAILROADING AIMED AT EVANDER. No mysterious notes, summons, chosen-one signs, or NPCs seeking him out for plot reasons. He only meets the plot if he goes to it. NPCs interact with him only as their own lives naturally would (bank, Academy staff, acquaintances, family in ordinary ways). Hooks/fate are for Ilsabet and Corwen only.
- RETCON 4 Rimewane opening: no anonymous note, no Halloway note, no Ilsabet sighting.

## Geography & scale (world = 1.5x Earth diameter; R 9,556 km; 166.8 km per degree; ~Earth-normal gravity)
- Map px = 30 km N-S; E-W stretched ~4.4x at Falariand's latitude.
- Wadun 76.7N, ~80 km from the Thornmarch border (war on the doorstep). Northern border/Ten Keeps ~82N, ~900 km north of Wadun (4-6 weeks overland).
- Crown lands ~630k km2 (France/Texas), ~2M people. Wadun ~60k people.
- Rime Duchies: 14 states, ~600k km2 total (much glacier), each 7k-55k km2, 20k-80k people each, ~600k total.
- Thornmarch: ~15 states, ~0.9-1M km2 total, each 12k-70k km2, 100k-400k people each, ~3M total.
- Silkwater enclave ~3-5k km2.
- Gravesmere: ~860k km2 island (measured), 65.6-73.7N. North tip is ~480 km S of Wadun in latitude only; it also lies ~13 deg of longitude west, so the true straight-line distance is ~710 km (4-6 days sail; more with spring ice). Royal barrows on its north shore; interior wild, unsettled, forbidden by Stormchurch.

## Small little-known ruins in the north (found by Evander's own research)
1. Kettlemoor Ring — Survey Folio 38 (Royal Survey, 664 S.C.). Stone ring + collapsed vault on a hill above Kettlemoor village, ~650 km N, north-central crown lands. Truth: Elder elven aurora-relay post; tower gone, small undercroft intact (3 rooms), sealed by frost-warded door (abjuration), contents: star-instruments, a few elven lenses, bones of 2 caretakers. Minor guardian: none, but floor partly collapsed into an ice cave with a wolverine den.
2. St. Ebba's Cell — library catalogue of lost works. Hermit-mage nun, d. ~400 yrs ago, tower burned, on an islet in Lake Corrach, NE crown lands ~720 km N, ~150 km from Thornmarch border. Assumed looted. Truth: looted above; cellar cistern under the islet (flooded/frozen) holds her waterproofed chest: half-ruined grimoire (abjuration/ice wards), letters. Locals (Corrach village) think islet is unlucky; ice fishers avoid it.
3. "The House in the Ice" — tavern rumor (old delver Gunnar Fisk, Rusty Gaff, paid 2 sh+drinks). Antlerfolk trader saw a small stone building exposed by last summer's melt, NW crown lands near Graveholt border, ~3 days N of the westernmost Keep (Keep Vardhal). Truth: Titanic-era giant's hunting shelter (single huge room), frozen giant-made tools, a frozen mammoth-like beast half in the ice; unstable meltwater; Rime raiders pass nearby. Others heard the rumor too (Fisk tells anyone who buys drinks).
4. The Stone Chair — Evander's childhood memory: Hollowpine Elkin hunters avoid a great "stone chair" deep in taiga north of the clan grounds (~500 km N of Wadun, NE). Truth: Titanic waystone/throne, small shrine, rimeglass deposits around it, spirit-haunted (an old elk-spirit guardian, not hostile if respected). Elkin consider it sacred — taking things will offend the clan.

## Small RECENT unknown ruins (Evander's research, 5 Rimewane; spent 6 sh in fees/donations -> purse ~62 cr 10 sh)
A. Aske's Mill — Academy Registry of Licensed Practitioners: Magister Corvin Aske, alchemist-conjurer, licence lapsed 795 S.C., last address "Aske's Mill, on the Brenna, ~90 km N of Wadun". Presumed dead. Truth: died 795 in a conjuration accident. Workshop (mill upper floor) sealed by his own frost-wards (still active, weakened). Inside: his body (frozen/mummified), notes, reagents (some spoiled, some valuable: ~15-25 cr), a small bound ice elemental (mephit-like) still in a cracked circle for 17 yrs — weak, furious, bargainable. Lower mill: empty, wolves sometimes den.
B. Watchtower Seven — Evander's copied survey sheets: coastal signal tower, marked "disused" since ~750, ~120 km W along coast. Truth: smugglers used it until ~802 (crew hanged in Wadun 803). Hidden cellar under hearthstone: 6 casks brandy, bolt of Weft silk (~10 cr), small strongbox (~4 cr coin, a ledger naming a Wadun customs officer still alive & now a Harbor Warden — Osric Pell).
C. Pelling — Cathedral parish registers: hamlet + chantry, "no souls remaining" after the White Cough outbreak of 776. ~260 km N, off the old north road. Truth: 8 collapsed houses, intact chantry & bell; last priest (Father Amund) hid silver altar plate (~20 cr) under the chancel floor; graveyard; a wolf pack winters in the ruins. Nothing supernatural. Disease long gone.
D. Harrowgate Lodge — Chancery land registry (fee 3 sh): hunting lodge of Baron Ulric Harrow, attainted for debt-fraud 781; reverted to crown, never sold (too remote). ~180 km NW in forest. Truth: bailiffs stripped it; roof half-fallen; Harrow hid a strongbox (~30 cr in old coin + family seal ring + letters) in the ice-house pit, under ice and sawdust. A charcoal-burner family (the Tolls: father, 2 sons) squats in the stables over winter.
Travel now (skis/sledge, snow): ~25-30 km/day.
