# GM LEDGER — Falariand campaign (private world state)

*Updated at the turn of the year, end of Deepdark 813 S.C. Previous update: end of Deepdark 812. Next update: end of Deepdark 814.*

## Rules of play
- Natural/real-world resolution, no stats. Outcomes from plausibility, skill, preparation, circumstance. Uncertain outcomes may be rolled honestly.
- World moves on its own. NPCs pursue goals offscreen. Player is NOT the center.
- Protagonists of the age: Ilsabet, Corwen, Aila, Tove Ranne, Brum Whitemaw, Ilvai Dhuun. Player = Evander, side figure. No protagonist is a villain; Wadun's view of the south is biased.
- NO PLOT HOOKS OR RAILROADING AIMED AT EVANDER. NPCs interact with him only as their own lives naturally would. He meets the plot only by going to it.
- Lethal choices kill. Bad plans fail. Generosity and threats both have consequences.
- The ledger is updated once per in-game year.
- Ruins vary: many hold nothing, or mundane danger. Don't repeat monster types.
- Bound elementals cannot smell or taste through their vessels (Snowflake senses light, sound, movement, cold, draught).

## Magic in the world (revised 813)
- **Practitioners** (real casters, all traditions): ~1 in 50, about 40,000 in the crown lands. **Knacks** (minor gifts): ~1 in 10.
- **Power is steep.** Most casters are 1st-3rd level equivalent. ~1 in 2,000 reach 5th+ (about 1,000 in the crown lands). Magister-level (9+) numbers a few hundred realm-wide. Archmagisters: 6.
- **Academy wizards** are an elite credential (~600 licensed; ~30 licensed a year). Ranks: Novice / Licentiate / Adept / Magister / Archmagister. Thousands more casters are unlicensed: hedge-wizards, private apprentices, and the traditions below.
- **Traditions:**
  - Stormchurch clerics (healing is real but limited; the white cough mostly resists it).
  - Cathedral paladins.
  - Shamans and wise-folk (druid-like; Kesa, Iska).
  - Sorcerers (born gift; must register with the Academy by Crown law).
  - Skalds and bards.
  - Wardens and rangers (the Keepers of the Lantern were these).
  - Artificers (the Rimeglass Guild of Hvel; instrument-smiths).
  - Warlocks (pact-makers; suspect and often illegal; distinct from compact-binding).
- **Falar's storm-gift is protected as unique:** calling true lightning out of the sky, from clear weather, at scale. Ordinary storm-sorcerers throw sparks. Whether the gift comes from blood, crown or god stays ambiguous.
- Armies carry many casters alongside muskets.
- Magic does not extend life by default.

## Peoples & lifespans
Humans 70-90. Antlerfolk (tundra reindeer-folk) 85-95. Elkin (taiga elk-folk) 130-150; antlers grow for life. Half-Elkin (Evander) ~100-120, aging slowly after ~25. Also: drow (Weft Company), Bearkin, Rime folk; giants and Titans of old; Elder elves (ruins only).

## SNOWFLAKE (bound ice elemental, frost-kin)
- **Bargain (9 Rimewane 812):**
  - Five winters bound, ending ~Rimewane 817; then north to the deep cold, where it chooses to stay or go.
  - It sees the world (not kept in darkness).
  - Never drained.
  - It MAY send its cold if Evander is in peril.
  - It must not harm him; chained forever if it causes his death.
  - Free if he dies otherwise.
  - Free and unbindable by him if he knowingly breaks the bargain.
- **Added terms:** broken-vessel term (only ice, rimeglass or clear glass; one moon to find a proper vessel); death term (if freed, goes north and harms no one unless someone tries to bind or harm it).
- **Registered:** middling since 15 Thawmonth 813; held lawfully at Evander's Adept rank; re-inspection annually (~Thawmonth 814).
- **Its choices:** acts inside Evander's line (death in the next breath), always for *him*, never as a weapon of war. It opposes his joining the army.
- **Evander's promises:** never take it to the weeping ice; never point the Lantern-ward at it; **never light fire near it.**
- **Grew greatly** in the winter of 813 at Station Fell (aurora, rimeglass lens). Sulks in summer heat.
- **Keeps hidden:** its true name; the full meaning of its humming (it answers the mother-ice, which is afraid of what lies under the giant).
- **Its view of Evander:** "keeps his word, careless with himself, I would like him not to die." Undecided about leaving.

## Salvage law
- Old ruins on waste land: salvage tolerated.
- Private land: the owner's.
- Treasure trove: Crown; a reporting finder is rewarded (Ravnsborg precedent: nine-tenths to the finder).
- Contraband: forfeit; a reporting finder gets ~1/3.
- Live claims: Academy seals and inventories; Church interdicts and parish property; administered Crown land.
